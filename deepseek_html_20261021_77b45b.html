About Sam

Sam founded Noctis Technologies around a clear belief: professional digital technology should be accessible to every business, not only those with large budgets. What started as a personal practice in web development has grown into a founder-led studio serving clients across Tamil Nadu, India and worldwide.

Originally from Dindigul and based in Tirupur for over five years, Sam brings a mix of technical depth and practical business thinking to every project. He studied at Karpagam Academy of Higher Education and has worked across entrepreneurship and blue-collar industries before focusing on digital work — a background that shapes how he approaches clients and their real-world constraints.

At Noctis, Sam leads web development, UI/UX and technical direction personally. He works hands-on with each project from the first conversation through launch and ongoing support — not handing off to junior teams or outsourcing. Every site Noctis ships is something he has reviewed and stands behind.
