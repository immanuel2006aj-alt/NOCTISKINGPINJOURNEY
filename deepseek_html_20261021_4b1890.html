Clear pricing. Clear expectations.

  • Website development starts from ₹1,999. Final pricing depends on project requirements, page count, functionality and complexity.
  • Domain & hosting registration, renewal and platform charges are separate third-party costs unless explicitly included in a project quote.
  • SEO services implement search-friendly structure and technical optimization. Specific search engine rankings are not guaranteed.
  • Payment terms are confirmed before development begins. For eligible projects, a 50% advance is required after scope and quote confirmation.
  • Revisions are included according to the agreed project package. Additional scope or major new features may be quoted separately.
  • Maintenance plans begin after launch and are billed monthly. They do not include new feature development unless separately agreed.

Questions about pricing.

Clear answers before you commit.

What is the starting price for a website?

Our basic business website package starts from ₹1,999. Final pricing depends on the number of pages, features, integrations and overall project scope.

Is the domain included in the website price?

No. Domain registration and renewal charges are separate unless specifically included in a project quote. Domain setup assistance is available as an add-on service.

How long does a website take to build?

Most websites are delivered in 3–15 business days depending on the scope, number of pages, features, content readiness and revision rounds.

What is included in the admin panel?

Admin panel availability depends on the package. Advanced and custom projects can include content management, product or service management, lead management and business dashboards based on project requirements.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes. Technical SEO, on-page SEO, local SEO and programmatic SEO architecture are available. We implement search-friendly structure but do not guarantee specific search rankings.

How does the payment process work?

Project payment terms are confirmed before development begins. For eligible projects, a 50% advance is collected after scope and quote confirmation, with the remaining balance due according to agreed project terms.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes. Maintenance plans start from ₹299/month and include content updates, bug fixes, backups and technical support depending on the plan selected.
