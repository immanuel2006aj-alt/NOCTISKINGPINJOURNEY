Privacy Policy

How Noctis Technologies collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Noctis Technologies ("Noctis", "we", "our", "us") is a digital technology studio based in Mangalam, Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data when you use www.noctistechnologies.gt.tc (the "Site") or contact us about our services.

We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India, and apply reasonable data protection practices aligned with global standards where relevant.

1. Data we collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Enquiry form data — name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, service requested, estimated budget, and the project description or message you send us.
  • Review submissions — your name, optional photo, business or organisation name, optional location, star rating and review text, when you choose to submit a review.
  • Communication data — anything you send via email, WhatsApp, or the site form.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data — browser type, device type, approximate location (derived from your IP address at country or city level), pages visited, and time of visit.
  • Cookies — small data files stored on your device. See Section 6 below.

1.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect or store payment card data on this Site.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information such as health, biometric, religious or caste data.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 years of age.

2. How we use your data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries — to understand your project, reply with quotes and communicate about work.
  • Service delivery — to provide the websites, designs, development or other services you engage us for.
  • Displaying reviews — when you submit a review and give permission to publish it, we display it on our site with your chosen details.
  • Improving our service — to understand how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • Legal and compliance — to meet record-keeping and other legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for marketing unrelated to your enquiry without your consent.

3. Legal basis for processing

Under the DPDP Act and applicable law, we process personal data on the basis of:

  • Your consent, given when you submit an enquiry form or review.
  • Legitimate use in connection with the services you have requested.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (Section 8).

4. Data sharing and third parties

We share data only where necessary, with the following categories of service providers, each of whom processes data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards:

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider, used to store enquiry data and reviews securely.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery provider.
  • Email and messaging services — used to receive and reply to enquiries.
  • Analytics — if enabled, a privacy-respecting analytics service to understand site usage.

We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose data if required to do so by law, court order or regulatory authority.

5. Data retention

  • Enquiry data — retained for up to 24 months after the last communication, unless a project or legal obligation requires longer retention.
  • Client and project data — retained for the duration of the engagement and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal and accounting obligations.
  • Review data — retained for as long as the review is published, or until you request its removal.

You may ask us to delete your data sooner (Section 8), and we will comply where no overriding legal obligation applies.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site uses minimal cookies and similar technologies:

  • Essential functionality — to remember UI preferences and help the site work correctly.
  • Anonymous analytics, if enabled — to understand general site usage; we prefer privacy-respecting analytics that do not track individual users.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect some site features.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Access controls and authentication for systems that store personal data.
  • Row-level security policies at the database layer to prevent unauthorised access.
  • Minimisation — collecting only the data necessary for the purpose.

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorised access to your data, contact us immediately at the address in Section 8.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — to know what personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Right to correction — to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to withdraw consent — to withdraw consent previously given.
  • Right to grievance redressal — to raise a complaint about our handling of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at mr.samimmanuel.j@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request". We respond within 30 days.

9. International transfers

Some of our service providers (such as hosting and database providers) may store data outside India. Where data is transferred internationally, we ensure reasonable safeguards are in place as required by applicable law.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be highlighted on this page. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
