About Milton

Milton keeps Noctis Technologies' financial operations organised and reliable. He works across financial planning, budgeting and coordination — making sure the studio operates on sound financial foundations while delivering quality work for clients.

Financial discipline matters in every project, from transparent quotes and milestone payments to final invoicing and handover. Milton supports these processes so that both Noctis and its clients have clear, honest financial coordination at every stage.

Alongside Sam and Esther, Milton is part of the core Noctis team — contributing the business and financial perspective that lets the studio stay focused on delivering strong digital work.
