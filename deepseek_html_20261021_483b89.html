About Esther

Esther works at the technical core of Noctis projects — the parts users don't see but depend on every day. She focuses on backend development: server-side logic, APIs, database structure and technical integrations that hold a modern web application together.

Her work ensures that front-end interfaces connect cleanly with the systems that store, retrieve and protect data — from contact forms and admin panels to product catalogues and business dashboards. She values reliability, security and clean architecture in everything she builds.

At Noctis, Esther collaborates directly with Sam Immanuel J on client projects, translating business requirements into functional backend systems. Every project benefits from her focus on making sure the technology underneath a website is as solid as the design on top.
